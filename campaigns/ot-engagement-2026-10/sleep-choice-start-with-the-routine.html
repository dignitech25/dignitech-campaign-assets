The starting point is what needs to happen around the bed, not simply which product to order.

Make the practical question clear

Settling, repositioning, transfers and assistance overnight can each raise different equipment questions. Looking at that sequence helps frame what you want to explore in a trial.

We can discuss the bed, mattress and accessories together, help organise the trial and stay involved as the setup is assessed. You remain the clinical lead, with the person's goals and home circumstances shaping the discussion.

Equipment is only one part of sleep and daily function. Our role is to help with the equipment questions, not promise that a new bed will resolve a sleep problem.
