A pressure-care mattress should not be chosen from a risk score or product brochure alone. The right surface depends on the person, how they move and transfer, their positioning and care needs, and the environment in which it will be used.

On 21 October, Sleep Choice and Forté Healthcare are bringing Craig Gorman to Brighton East for Improving Client Safety Through Clinical Mattress Selection, a practical in-person session for occupational therapists.

Static, hybrid or powered?

Craig will unpack how different support surfaces work, where each fits, and the clinical factors that should shape the choice. The session covers pressure-care fundamentals, pressure-injury prevention, practical examples and Q&A across community, aged care and acute settings.

We are proud to work closely with Forté. Their Australian-made range is best in class, with genuine depth across static, hybrid and powered support surfaces.

Through Sleep Choice, OTs can arrange flexible in-home trials across the Forté mattress range. If the first configuration does not give the clinical team enough confidence, we can help adjust the setup and keep the assessment moving.
