Static, hybrid and powered mattresses can all sound credible on paper. Seeing the surfaces together makes the differences, trade-offs and clinical questions much easier to examine.

Sleep Choice and Forté Healthcare are bringing Craig Gorman to Brighton East on 21 October for a practical, in-person session created for occupational therapists.

Clinical mattress selection, made tangible

Craig will explore pressure-care fundamentals, pressure-injury prevention and how different support-surface technologies apply across community, aged care and acute settings. The session includes real-world clinical applications, discussion and Q&A.

Forté is the clinical mattress range we are most excited to work with and stand behind. You will be able to compare the range in person and put detailed questions directly to their team.

Through Sleep Choice, OTs can then arrange flexible seven-night home trials across the Forté range. It is a practical way to assess comfort, positioning, transfers, usability and overall suitability in the client's own environment before finalising a recommendation.
