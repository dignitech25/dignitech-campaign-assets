On 21 October, Sleep Choice and Forté Healthcare are bringing Craig Gorman to Brighton East for Improving Client Safety Through Clinical Mattress Selection, a practical in-person training session created for occupational therapists.

Clinical mattress selection, made practical

Craig will cover pressure-care fundamentals, pressure-injury prevention and how static, hybrid and powered support surfaces apply across community, aged care and acute settings. The session connects the technology to everyday clinical decision-making, with practical examples, discussion and Q&A.

Forté is the clinical mattress range we are most excited to stand behind. The range is broad, the products are Australian made, and their team brings serious depth in pressure care and support-surface design.

Sleep Choice can then help translate that learning into practice. We can arrange flexible seven-night home trials across the Forté range, giving the client and OT the opportunity to assess the selected mattress in the environment where it will actually be used.
