For a child or young person with complex physical support needs, the bed decision affects more than comfort.

It has to work for positioning, transfers, care routines, the people providing assistance and the other equipment already in the room.

Test the whole overnight setup

A mattress may feel appropriate while the bed height makes a transfer harder. A side or accessory may help one task and obstruct another. The support person may not have the access they need once everything is in the room.

A useful home trial brings the person, support routine, room and equipment together before the final decision.

1.Can the person be positioned and repositioned as intended?
2.Can family or support workers provide assistance from the space available?
3.Does the bed, mattress and transfer equipment work as one setup?
4.What needs to change before the OT recommends the next step?
