Make the trial answer a real question

A bed or mattress can look suitable during a daytime assessment and still leave important questions unanswered through ordinary overnight use.

Defining those questions first gives the trial a clearer purpose and gives the OT more useful observations to bring back into the equipment decision.

1.What overnight task or concern needs to be understood?
2.What should be observed during ordinary use at home?
3.Which configuration variables need to be compared?
4.What evidence would help support the next decision?
