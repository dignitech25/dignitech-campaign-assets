For a person with complex physical support needs, choosing a mattress in isolation can miss the harder part of the brief.

The bed height, positioning, transfer equipment, room access and the way assistance is provided all have to work as one setup.

Test the interaction, not only the individual products

A mattress may support one goal while the bed height makes a transfer harder. An accessory may help positioning but obstruct access for a family member or support worker.

A useful home trial brings those dependencies into the same discussion before the final equipment recommendation.

1.Can the person be positioned and repositioned as intended?
2.Can assistance be provided safely from the space available?
3.Do the bed, mattress and transfer equipment work together in the actual room?
